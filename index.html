Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a haircut?

Services start from ₦5,000, depending on what you're getting. Message us or call/WhatsApp for exact pricing.

Do I need to book ahead, or can I walk in?

Walk in any time we're open, or reach out first — call or WhatsApp 0916 890 0095, or DM @329groominglounge on Instagram.

Where are your locations?

64 Oba Molaja Ogunlewe Road, Igbogbo, Ikorodu, Lagos.

Do you operate during public holidays?

Mon–Wed 9am–11pm. Thu–Sun 9am–2am.

Where can I find your products?

Right here at the lounge — we stock skincare and haircare products in-store. Message us on Instagram to check what's currently available.

How do I apply to become a barber?

Send us a DM on Instagram @329groominglounge with a bit about your experience, and we'll get back to you.
